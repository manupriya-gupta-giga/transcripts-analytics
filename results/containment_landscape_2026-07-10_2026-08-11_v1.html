How to read this

Each bar is one bucket — an intent crossed with one variable-store attribute value. Wide, short bars are the problem: lots of conversations, low containment. Wide, tall bars are the agent doing its job at scale. The dashed line is the expected containment target, fixed at 82%; bars below it are underperforming buckets.

  • Containment = 100% − escalation rate; a conversation counts as escalated when the agent filed a create_ticket for a human team.
  • Buckets under 200 conversations are folded into the Other bar (hover it for the count). Bars are sorted widest-first.
  • Same basis as the escalation heatmaps: Jul 10 – Aug 11 2026, Aug 4 excluded (inaccurate), conversations with ≤1 message excluded, intent native from Aug 8 and tag-derived before.
