Where chats escalate to humans

How to read this

Noise filter: conversations with at most one message (all roles counted) are excluded — 5,639 of 83,796 (6.7%); none of them had escalated.

Every ticket is bucketed by the intent the analysis pipeline assigned (rows, hottest first) and by four account attributes captured in the conversation's variable store (one heatmap per attribute). Cell color is the selected metric; hover any cell for ticket counts, escalations, rate, and SOP-gap count. Rate cells with fewer than 10 tickets stay uncolored — too small to trust a percentage.

  • Escalated — the agent called create_ticket, i.e. filed a support ticket for a human team.
  • SOP-gap escalation (proxy) — an escalation that shows no sign of a specific matching rule: ticket filed under the catch-all general_escalation tag, or the conversation was flagged is_out_of_scope, or intent classified OutOfDomain. Policy-driven escalations (hardship, fraud, eviction language …) carry their specific scenario tag and are excluded.
  • Intent — native from the analysis pipeline where available (Aug 8 onward); earlier tickets get their intent derived from the first analysis tag the current tag→intent taxonomy recognizes (45,415 of 83,796 derived). Shape-of-conversation tags (GreetingOnly, RequestHumanSupport) never derive an intent. (no intent) = neither available — mostly greeting-only or untagged sessions.
  • (none) means the attribute was never populated in that conversation's variable store — typically short sessions that ended before account lookup.
