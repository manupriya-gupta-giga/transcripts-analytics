Gaps mean no data for that day. Days are US-Eastern; chats count only conversations with ≥1 customer message (verified against the ops Total Chats sheet within ~0.2% before user exclusions). Jul 1–9 and the evening hours of Jun 30 (Jun 30 window) come from ticket metadata, which has neither customer IDs nor messages — the user exclusion and engagement filters do not apply there. In the Contribution view, segments are volume-weighted (intent's escalated chats ÷ all chats) and stack to the day's overall rate; "Other" covers chats outside the top 8 intents.
